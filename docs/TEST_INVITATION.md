# Invitation to try Headache Diary Lab / 头痛日记体验邀请 — 0.6.1

## 中文邀请

**欢迎帮助我们把头痛日记做得更容易理解、更容易使用。**

Headache Diary Lab是一款以患者为中心的头痛记录实验原型，将每日状态、发作、实际用药与随访反馈放在一起，支持八种界面语言。0.6.1新增逐条反馈历史，以及包含方案调整和患者反馈的医生摘要；Windows x64便携包提供一键启动。

如果你对患者记录工具、医学沟通、多语言界面或可访问性有兴趣，欢迎体验并告诉我们哪里难懂、难找或难操作。无需软件工程经验，也不要求提供个人健康信息。可以花约15–20分钟选择几项任务，随时停止或跳过；没有完成次数或持续打卡要求。

**本次只使用应用自带的合成病例或你编写的虚构示例。** 这是软件可用性体验，不是临床试验或诊疗服务，不用于判断个人病情，也不承诺改善头痛或治疗效果。医生身份、治疗方案、消息提醒与机构共享均为模拟；自动语音识别需自行接入服务，手机服务、PWA安装和小程序仍有待部署或验证事项。

你可以尝试：

- 按说明启动，填写并修改一条虚构日记，再停止和重新打开。
- 查看两条历史反馈，生成摘要，判断日期、用药与建议来源是否容易理解。
- 在熟悉的语言下检查文字、按钮、术语及手机宽度布局；指出译文不自然或含义不清的地方。

## English invitation

**Help make a headache diary easier to understand and use.**

Headache Diary Lab is a patient-centered research prototype bringing daily status, headache episodes, actual medication use and follow-up feedback together in eight interface languages. Version 0.6.1 adds individual feedback history and clinician-readable summaries that include treatment-plan changes and patient feedback. A Windows x64 portable bundle offers one-click startup.

If you are interested in patient recording tools, medical communication, language accessibility or usability, we would welcome your observations about what is confusing, difficult to find or hard to use. No software-engineering experience or personal health information is needed. You can spend approximately 15–20 minutes on a few tasks, stop at any time or skip anything; there is no completion or daily-use requirement.

**Use only the included synthetic case or fictional examples.** This is a software-usability exercise, not a clinical trial or medical service. It is not for evaluating your health and does not promise headache relief or treatment benefit. Clinician identities, treatment plans, reminders and institution delivery are simulated. Automatic speech transcription requires a configured service; mobile deployment, PWA installation and mini-program integration still have pending work or validation.

You could try:

- Starting from the guide, saving and editing a fictional diary entry, then stopping and reopening the app.
- Reviewing two historical feedback entries and generating a summary, checking whether dates, medication and advice provenance are understandable.
- Reviewing a familiar language, labels, buttons, clinical terms and narrow-window layout, and pointing out unnatural or ambiguous wording.
